Delete selected nodule?

    

    
This removes only the selected nodule across all slices and planes, including any disconnected regions assigned to it. Other nodules, the CT and previous revisions are retained. Undo restores the mask and nodule ID.

Completion locks this saved mask and enables case export. Reopen or Undo completion to edit again. Reviewer approval is a separate step.
